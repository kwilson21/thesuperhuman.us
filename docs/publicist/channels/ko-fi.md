# Ko-fi (on hold)

Status: no link goes on the website or in posts until the owner answers these. The
footer link from PR #91 was closed for that reason; its branch is kept.

The page, https://ko-fi.com/kazonwilson, supports all of the owner's work, software
and audio alike. Questions for the owner:

1. **What does support pay for?** For example time on open projects (Tally,
   kaillera-next), hosting and service costs, or future music releases.
2. **A goal?** Ko-fi can show a funding goal. A concrete one (a monthly hosting
   amount, the next release) is easier to support than an open request.
3. **One-off tips only, or a membership tier?** A membership implies a regular
   benefit that has to be delivered on schedule.
4. **How does it relate to the paid audio services?** The site already lists fixed
   audio prices. Ko-fi should read as support for the work, not a second price
   list or a cheaper route to a mix.
5. **What do supporters get?** Updates, early looks at videos or releases, a
   thank-you credit on the site (only with their consent), or nothing beyond
   thanks.

Once answered, the publicist drafts: the Ko-fi page text (about, goal, thank-you
message) as a checklist for the owner to paste in; the website link (reopening
PR #91 or a fresh one); and the profile and pinned-post placements below. Public copy says "support my work" and never mentions
employment status.

  structured-data profile links (drafted in PR #91, closed until the plan
  exists). Because every post links to a journal page on the site, readers who want to help can always
  find it. A later option, if wanted: a short support line on the Building project
  pages and the Old News release page.
- **Profiles:** in the pinned Bluesky post, in LinkedIn's contact info (it allows
  several website links) and in the X bio before it goes quiet.
- **Posts:** routine posts keep their one link, to the site. At most one explicit
  support post a month, drafted only when there is a concrete milestone to point to,
  and queued like any other post for the owner's approval.

  on Ko-fi").
