# Website direction

Date: 2026-09-09; positioning refined 2026-09-10.
Status: Agreed direction. The audit and primary-page implementation have since
been completed locally; see the content model and system plan for current scope.

## Purpose

Represent who I am today, demonstrate what I can do, and create room for the work
and relationships I want to explore next.

The website represents Kazon's interests, ideas, current projects, and demonstrated
abilities. It should make complex work understandable and credible while creating
opportunities compatible with his interests and preferred way of working.
Employment and contracting are possible outcomes, rather than its sole purpose.

Past experience establishes credibility without defining future direction. The
site should invite work Kazon wants to explore, not simply attract more of the work
he has already done. His current direction is software engineering focused on AI-assisted building.
He enjoys shaping solutions, exploring, prototyping, and iterating toward something
people can use. Collaborators can bring the idea; ownership of the original idea
is not a requirement. Backend and data experience supplies evidence rather than
defining the work the website should attract.

## Audiences and outcomes

- A curious visitor understands who Kazon is, the kind of work he does, what he is
  currently working on, and how to get in touch. No professional intent is needed.
- Someone screening or recommending him can assemble an accurate, shareable
  profile of his experience, strengths, accomplishments, and working preferences.
- A hiring manager or potential client can assess relevance, inspect evidence,
  and decide whether to initiate a conversation. The site should answer important
  preliminary questions without pretending to replace all further evaluation.
- Potential collaborators can recognize shared interests and reasons to connect.

## Opportunity positioning

Independent contracting is a preference; compelling full-time opportunities are
also welcome. Autonomy over time, meaningful ownership, choice of collaborators,
and interest in the work matter more than the engagement label alone.

This agreed direction supersedes the contractor-only positioning in CLAUDE.md
where it conflicts. The owner approved the corresponding copy changes on
September 10. Existing privacy, publication, truthful-claims, and engagement
safeguards continue to apply. Public copy and agent guidance use the same direction.

## Information architecture

The agreed primary areas are Home, Work, Building, Writing, and About. Their exact
page boundaries, navigation treatment, and content allocation will follow the
audit. Audio is an existing dedicated destination whose relationship to these
areas should be assessed during that audit.

## Expression and evidence

Help visitors understand through evidence they can see, hear, or interact with.
Avoid walls of text, unnecessary repetition, and explanations that assume readers
already understand technically complex work.

Use concise prose alongside purposeful visuals, sound, and motion. Choose media
for what it explains, not as decoration or a requirement on every page. Preserve
room for depth when a reader wants it. A visual restyling is not inherently needed.

Specific patterns such as annotated examples, comparisons, diagrams, interactive
demonstrations, and audio comparisons are candidates, not selected components.
The audit must establish which actually serve the available content. Accessibility,
user control of playback, reduced-motion behavior, and performance belong in the
evaluation of any proposed media treatment.

Separate presentation gaps from evidence gaps. Better layout cannot substitute
for a missing accomplishment, artifact, or supported result. Identify material
that needs to be created, not just existing content that needs to move.

Future open-source contributions may provide evidence of technical judgment and
collaboration. They are an interest, not an existing credential or a committed
roadmap. Their absence does not prevent a useful website now.

## Exploration and truthful representation

Make current interests and experiments visible without presenting possibilities
as commitments. Distinguish exploration, implementation, verification, and public
availability. Preserve the existing publication policy and source authority.

Career exploration can remain private. The website need not disclose every
uncertainty or intermediate thought. Compatible conversations and collaborations
are valuable outcomes alongside employment or contracting inquiries.

## Maintainability

Establish a small, documented design system from the existing website and its
conventions. Make it clear where content, page composition, shared presentation,
and behavior belong. Reuse existing foundations before introducing abstractions
or dependencies. New updates should follow discoverable examples rather than
inventing another convention.

The component inventory, directory changes, and tooling remain undecided pending
the audit. Neither shadcn adoption nor a separately packaged component library is
an agreed requirement.

## Process and next deliverable

1. Discuss intent and document agreed principles in this brief.
2. Audit the existing website against the brief.
3. Choose a concrete presentation and content model from the findings.
4. Plan and implement approved changes in reviewable steps.

The audit should assess page purpose, audience questions, discoverability,
repetition, reading burden, supporting evidence, available media, accessibility,
and maintenance friction. Record what to keep, clarify, consolidate, or create,
with reasons and affected pages. Identify claims needing confirmation and new
assets needing owner input. Evaluate whether current positioning attracts the
kind of future Kazon wants to explore.

Do not select a showcase project, prescribe a component system, or treat an
implementation proposal as accepted before these findings inform the choice.

## References and their role

- [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/docs): inspiration for owned, composable code
  with consistent interfaces and discoverable conventions.
- [SmoothUI](https://smoothui.dev/): inspiration for showing available components,
  larger compositions, and interaction examples.
- [Human–AI interaction principles](https://syntaxstream.substack.com/p/42-principles-for-designing-humanai):
  inspiration for inspectable evidence, user agency, recoverability, and appropriate
  autonomy. Apply these to maintaining the site without assuming a visitor-facing
  AI feature is needed.
- [Designing Your Life, Bill Burnett and Dave Evans](https://designingyour.life/books-designing-life-original-book/):
  career inspiration through exploration and experimentation. The website
  principles above are an application discussed with the owner, not claims that
  the book prescribes a website design.
