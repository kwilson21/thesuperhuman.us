D7.1 · Your call 13 · /services

How should the three “Before we start.” steps sit on /services?

Hallmark flagged the steps as a “3-column feature grid, numbered-step variant” (ws-4, punch list #29) and suggested a single-column numbered list. The verifier called that overstated. What the captures do show: at 1280 each step gets a 196px column, so the descriptions run 12 to 22 characters a line over up to four lines, and the list draws its own rule 37px under the section’s rule, so the section opens with two lines.

Where it was decided: docs/prelaunch-qa-2026-09-29-services-redesign.md:7-8 (you chose Direction A from rendered mockups on September 29 and asked that the page match the rest of the site; the scope lists the “Before we start.” steps) and :25 (the steps are an ordered list). The heading-left, content-right grid is shared with /audio/services (src/styles/service-pages.css, .service-before, 1fr 2fr). The three columns themselves are /services only (src/layouts/ServiceSheet.astro, .software-steps). Options C and D keep the shared grid. Option B moves the heading above the steps, as the print sheet and the page at 800px wide and below already do.

D7.1-AKeep as is

Live now
What changes
Nothing. Three equal columns of numeral, title and description in the right two thirds, with hairlines between them. Phones stack the steps.
Why it could be better
It is what you approved with Direction A. The three short steps read left to right as a sequence and the whole section stays one compact band (251px tall at 1280).
Trade-off
12 to 22 characters a line (the audit’s own reading range is 45 to 75), “Review the first milestone” wraps, the section opens with two rules, and the steps look like the feature rows Hallmark looks for.
Effort · files
None.

D7.2 · Your call 14 · /services and /audio/services

The audio label is built from the recording title at src/pages/audio/services.astro:24. From 651 to 800px wide the evidence labels already sit under their headings; at 650 and below a label sits beside its heading only when it fits.

D7.2-BBeside when it fits, under when it does not

Recommended

Why this one: it fixes the one real defect, the wrapped audio heading, without a copy change, and keeps the approved label-on-the-right look everywhere it fits.

Phone at 390: same arrangement. The labels already sit under their headings there (see D7.2-A); the gap under each evidence heading gets about 5px tighter (one .3rem gap where today there are two). A heading never shrinks. If its label fits beside it on the same line, the label stays on the right as approved; if not, the whole label moves under the heading. Today that changes only “Hear the difference.”: two lines become one, and the label becomes one line under it.
Why it could be better
No copy change, and the layout follows the content: a longer example title later cannot break the heading again. One rule replaces the two width-specific overrides in service-pages.css. The audio hero gets 9px shorter at 1280 (584px against 593px).
Trade-off
At 1280 the two heads on /audio/services differ (label under for the player, on the right for the prices). Where a label lands depends on its length. Between about 560 and 650px wide the price note would sit on the right, where today it drops under; from about 425 to 440px wide “Lyft · Associate Tools” drops under its heading, where today it just fits beside it.
Effort · files

Guideline check
