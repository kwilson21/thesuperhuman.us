Moving money between your own accounts, or getting paid back, isn't spending. So Tally shouldn't count it.

Excluded transactions already stayed out of spending, safe to spend and the Needs category count. Now you decide which ones: one "Exclude from budget" toggle in a transaction's edit panel. Transactions Jev, Tally's AI classifier, flags as a transfer or reimbursement start excluded, but Jev never overrides a choice a person made.

The first panel was too cluttered, so I picked a simpler layout from three mockups. Claude Code built it, including a browser test that excludes a transaction and checks Home.

Built and tested on demo data: https://thesuperhuman.us/building/tally#tally-exclusions
