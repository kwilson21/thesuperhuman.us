An audit of my site against a design standard flagged choices I had made on purpose. I compared each with a few alternatives and made 42 picks.

The standard is Hallmark, open source, by Together AI and the Hallmark contributors (MIT license). The audit only reported. Its real defects were fixed: faint form field edges, pages that scrolled sideways, and key links below the first screen.

Two of my picks are in the image: service doors beside the headline, and a 404 page with its words first.

Hallmark is now the design standard for my UI work, and every build checks six of its rules. Claude Code built it to my design, and I reviewed it. Live on the site.
