Filing Costco as Groceries in Tally no longer quietly makes a rule. Save files the purchase, then Tally asks "Always use Groceries for Costco?", and only Yes makes the rule. Here I tap Not now. I decided Tally never makes a rule unless someone says yes first. Built with Claude Code.
