A design audit found Tally's Settings was 3,554 pixels tall on a phone, with two ways of saving.

It also found Tally asking about a suggested category in three different ways. From drawings beside today's screens, I picked fixes.

Settings is now a contents page: each section opens its own page, and a switch saves when you flip it.

A transaction's edit panel leads with its category, in two columns, and folds it to one line once it's set. Exclude from budget is a switch.

A suggested new category is one card in Settings and Suggestions, and a short version of it in Organize.

Claude Code built it to my design, and I reviewed it. Built and tested on demo data.
