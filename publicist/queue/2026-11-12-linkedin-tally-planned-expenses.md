On Tally's demo, Safe to spend dropped from $343 to $73 with two planned expenses waiting.

My earlier budgeting app let me plan for one-off costs, and Tally couldn't. Now Bills has Plan an expense: what it's for, the amount and the month. Safe to spend sets that amount aside for the whole month.

Linking the payment on the plan's page ends the set-aside, and the payment counts once, in its own category. A plan still unpaid after its month stays set aside until Bills' question is answered: move it to this month, or drop it.

I picked each screen from drawings. Claude Code built it, and I reviewed it. Built and tested on demo data.
