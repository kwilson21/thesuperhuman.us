Changing a budget in Settings felt odd. My earlier budgeting app and Mint change it where you see the bars, so Tally does too.

Tapping a budget on Home opens a sheet with the money input from my earlier app: round ±$1 buttons, cent arrows, and chips to round up or use last month's spending. For quick changes, "Adjust" puts a minus and a plus on every row, and each tap saves the budget at the next round $10.

I picked that from three layouts drawn side by side, because buttons on every row looked cluttered on a phone. It all works without JavaScript. Built and tested on demo data: https://thesuperhuman.us/building/tally#tally-adjust-budgets
