A bill paid in two halves never showed Paid in Tally. Now several payments can pay one bill. In the clip, on demo data, Rent reads "Part paid: $600 of $1,200" with Link another payment. I picked the rule: a month is Paid once its payments reach 90% of the bill. Built with Claude Code.
