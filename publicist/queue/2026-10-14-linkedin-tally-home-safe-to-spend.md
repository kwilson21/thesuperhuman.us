Tally is meant for people who gave up on other budgeting apps, and an audit against its design language found Home burying its one number.

On a phone, the demo's Things to try and a large month title came first, and "Safe to spend" started halfway down the screen. I saw each fix next to today's Home and picked there.

Claude Code built them: the month is now a small heading, the number sits on a phone's first screen, and Things to try moves below the budget list. Budget bars are thinner, and an over-budget row says by how much. Built and tested on demo data.
